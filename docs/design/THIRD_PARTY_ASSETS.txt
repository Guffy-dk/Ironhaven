Ironhaven — third-party art sources

Crab anatomical base and PBR maps: Grab3D
https://grab3d.com/models/animals/crab/
https://grab3d.com/dl/crab/crab.glb

Horror monster anatomical base and PBR maps: Grab3D
https://grab3d.com/models/creatures/horror-monster/
https://grab3d.com/dl/horror-monster/horror-monster.glb

Both source packages are published under CC0 1.0 Universal:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode

Grab3D origin disclosure: AI-assisted. Base mesh and textures generated
with Meshy, then cleaned, scaled, textured and checked in Blender.
Ironhaven adaptations: anatomical cropping, species scale, UV-preserving
near/distance LODs, coastal color treatment, barnacles, kelp and animation.
No endorsement by the source authors is implied.

The source repository contains original license text, upstream manifests,
SHA-256 receipts and reproducible Blender adaptation scripts.
The workshop and creature concept illustrations and coastal surface atlas
were generated with OpenAI ImageGen. Concepts are not in-game screenshots.
