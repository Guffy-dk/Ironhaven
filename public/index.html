EXPEDITION BRIEFING
FIELD GUIDE / 02

Keep the expedition supplied.

Survive eight swarms, build an evacuation pad, and complete the final launch defense. The starting colony already has two working production lines.

01 / Automate

Drill → conveyor → smelter → core. A smelter handles both iron and copper ore. Assemblers turn two plates into one gear. Cyan IN ports accept items; gold OUT ports deliver them. Press R to rotate the blueprint. To rotate an existing conveyor, exit build mode and press R over it.

02 / Dispatch

Transfer relays load from a conveyor behind them into a machine ahead; Return is on the right. Dispatch hubs take input from behind, with Machine left, Forward ahead, Return right. Click Program flow to set quantities. Default 1 / 1 / 0. For three equal stations use 1:2, 1:1, 1:0 along the bus.

03 / Route

Hold left click and drag a conveyor along its arrow. Press R or Shift+R to set a corner while dragging. Release to build. Use Shift-click or Shift-drag to route around obstacles. Q or Esc cancels the preview.

03 / Fortify

Turrets load twelve shots per gear. Every building supports ten upgrades. B shows bottlenecks; I opens production statistics. Higher levels cost more, and advanced upgrades require steel and circuits. Amber or red ammo markers warn of low or empty magazines. Damaged buildings show health bars; repair with 9.

04 / Relocate

Dismantle with 0 to pack a building into your inventory. Hotbar numbers show how many you hold. Placement uses packed buildings before raw materials. Damage, ammo and upgrades are preserved.

05 / Explore

WASD follows screen directions, including W+A diagonals. The camera follows you automatically. Arrow keys or the minimap enable free camera; Space resumes following. Hold Shift to sprint. Engineers fire automatically and respawn after eight seconds.

06 / Cooperate

Open your current world to LAN from the menu. One host and three guests share the colony. Each engineer has a personal building inventory. The host controls time, saves and restarting. Keep the host’s game open.

Sawmills near trees supply wood. Generators consume wood and add eighteen power. Under power shortage, the newest machines stop. The swarm timer flashes red in the final twenty seconds. Worlds save locally every thirty seconds. Before each swarm, a rolling checkpoint is stored; retry it from the menu or defeat screen. Checkpoints are independent of autosave. Recently hit buildings cannot be relocated or dismantled for five seconds. Copper drills and smelters supply circuit assemblers. Steel foundries and component factories supply advanced upgrades and evacuation. Warehouses expand storage. Drag to select buildings, then press M to move them. Invalid moves are blocked; hold Shift to pack invalid buildings after confirmation. Export/import backups from the menu. Old saves migrate in the same browser storage.
