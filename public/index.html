FIELD GUIDE / 02

Keep the expedition supplied.

Survive 25 swarms. Core hunters pursue the command core and other hostiles target your production. Field training guides you through building your own iron and gear lines. Start with the buildings in your inventory.

01 / Automate

Drill → conveyor → smelter → core. A smelter handles both iron and copper ore. Assemblers turn two plates into one gear. Cyan arrows point into machines. Gold arrows show where items leave. Select a transfer relay or dispatch hub for its port labels. Press R to rotate the blueprint. To rotate an existing conveyor, exit build mode and press R over it.

02 / Dispatch

Transfer relays load from a conveyor behind them into a machine ahead; Return is on the right. Dispatch hubs take input from behind, with Machine left, Forward ahead, Return right. Click Program flow to set quantities. Default 1 / 1 / 0. For three equal stations use 1:2, 1:1, 1:0 along the bus.

03 / Route

Hold left click and drag a conveyor along its arrow. Press R or Shift+R to set a corner while dragging. Release to build. Use Shift-click or Shift-drag to route around obstacles. Q or Esc cancels the preview.

03 / Fortify

Sentry turrets use no ammo. Gun turrets take 16 shots per gear and can be upgraded. Mortars cover wide groups, while pulse turrets use shorter-range bursts. Every building supports ten upgrades. B shows bottlenecks; I opens production statistics. Higher levels cost more, and advanced upgrades require steel and circuits. Amber or red ammo markers warn of low or empty magazines. Damaged buildings show health bars; open Defense with X and press 6 to repair.

04 / Relocate

Press F and click a building to recover 80% of construction and upgrade materials. Click a tree to harvest it, or hold Shift and drag to clear an area. Starter buildings remain available in the hotbar.

05 / Explore

WASD and the arrow keys pan the camera. Hold Shift to pan faster, or click the minimap to jump. Z, X, C and V open build categories. Use the displayed numbers to pick a building.

06 / Cooperate

Open your current world to LAN from the menu. One host and three guests share the colony. Each player has a personal building inventory. The host controls time, saves and restarting. Keep the host’s game open.

Sawmills near trees supply wood without using grid power. Generators consume wood and add eighteen power. Under power shortage, the newest machines stop. The swarm timer flashes red in the final twenty seconds. The HUD shows the active and upcoming round bonuses. Worlds save locally every thirty seconds. Before each swarm, a rolling checkpoint is stored; retry it from the menu or defeat screen. Checkpoints are independent of autosave. Recently hit buildings cannot be relocated or dismantled for five seconds. Copper drills and smelters supply circuit assemblers. Steel foundries and component factories supply advanced defenses. Coal mines supply coal generators through colony storage. Warehouses store belt deliveries locally. Select the output material or choose Adjacent machine recipe to supply a neighboring factory from that warehouse. Use R to change the output direction. Deliver materials to the core to make them available for construction. Drag to select buildings, then press M to move them. Invalid moves are blocked; hold Shift to pack invalid buildings after confirmation. Export/import backups from the menu. Old saves migrate in the same browser storage.
