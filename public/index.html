FIELD GUIDE / 02

Industry is your defense.

Survive eight swarms, build an evacuation pad, and complete the final launch defense. The starting colony already has two working production lines.

01 / Automate

Drill → conveyor → smelter → core. Assemblers turn two plates into one gear. Cyan IN ports accept items; gold OUT ports deliver them. Press R over a conveyor to rotate it in place.

02 / Route

Place a conveyor, then Shift-click an endpoint to build a smart route from your last placement. Or Shift-drag to preview a new route and release to build it. Routes turn corners and avoid obstacles. Q or Esc exits building.

03 / Fortify

Turrets load twelve shots per gear. Every building supports ten upgrades. Higher levels cost more, and advanced upgrades require steel and circuits. Amber or red ammo markers warn of low or empty magazines. Damaged buildings show health bars; repair with 9.

04 / Relocate

Dismantle with 0 to pack a building into your inventory. Hotbar numbers show how many you hold. Placement uses packed buildings before raw materials. Damage, ammo and upgrades are preserved.

05 / Explore

WASD follows screen directions, including W+A diagonals. The camera follows you automatically. Arrow keys or the minimap enable free camera; Space resumes following. Hold Shift to sprint. Engineers fire automatically and respawn after eight seconds.

06 / Cooperate

Open your current world to LAN from the menu. One host and three guests share the colony. Each engineer has a personal building inventory. The host controls time, saves and restarting. Keep the host’s game open.

Sawmills near trees supply wood. Generators consume wood and add eighteen power. Under power shortage, the newest machines stop. The swarm timer flashes red in the final twenty seconds. Worlds save locally every thirty seconds. Copper drills and smelters supply circuit assemblers. Steel foundries and component factories supply advanced upgrades and evacuation. Warehouses expand storage. Drag to select buildings, then press M to move them. Invalid moves are blocked; hold Shift to pack invalid buildings after confirmation. Export/import backups from the menu. Old saves migrate in the same browser storage.
