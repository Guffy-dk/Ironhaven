FIELD GUIDE / 02

Field guide

Keep the lighthouse standing through 25 swarms. Use your starter buildings to set up iron and gear production, then defend the coast. Training walks you through the first production lines and defenses. Spitters appear from swarm four; an Abyss walker joins every fifth swarm.

01 / Automate

Drill → conveyor → smelter → lighthouse. A smelter handles both iron and copper ore. Assemblers turn two plates into one gear. Cyan arrows point into machines. Gold arrows show where items leave. Select a transfer relay or dispatch hub for its port labels. Press R to rotate the blueprint. To rotate an existing conveyor, exit build mode and press R over it.

02 / Dispatch

Transfer relays load from a conveyor behind them into a machine ahead; Return is on the right. Dispatch hubs take input from behind, with Machine left, Forward ahead, Return right. Click Program flow to set quantities. Default 1 / 1 / 0. For three equal stations use 1:2, 1:1, 1:0 along the bus.

03 / Route

Hold left click and drag a conveyor along its arrow. Press R or Shift+R to set a corner while dragging. Release to build. Use Shift-click or Shift-drag to route around obstacles. Q or Esc cancels the preview.

04 / Fortify

Sentry turrets use no ammo. Gun turrets take 16 shots per gear and can be upgraded. Mortars cover wide groups, while pulse turrets use shorter-range bursts. Industry, the lighthouse and advanced defenses support ten upgrades; the sentry stays at a fixed level. B shows bottlenecks; I opens production statistics. Higher levels cost more, and advanced upgrades require steel and circuits. Amber or red ammo markers warn of low or empty magazines. Damaged buildings show health bars; open Defense with X and press 6 to repair.

05 / Relocate

Press F and click a building to recover 80% of construction and upgrade materials. Click a tree to harvest it, or hold Shift and drag to clear an area. Starter buildings remain available in the hotbar.

06 / Lighthouse

On a new island, the lighthouse automatically turns toward the incoming coastal attack and lights a broad sector. Its upgrades improve reach, sector width and turning speed. Press L or Aim light, then click the map for manual control; Resume auto light restores tracking. Escape or right-click cancels. The lantern turns gradually and stops while paused. Illuminated hostiles can be targeted 20% beyond normal defense range. Shore runners briefly slow by 15% without changing their approach direction; Drowned are drawn toward the light source, including a nearby Harbor lamp. Build Harbor lamps with C then 3 for independent local light. Each costs 12 wood, 35 plates and 3 gears, consumes 3 power, covers six tiles and has only 75 HP. Creatures can target and destroy them. Sustained light exposes Shell guard armor and the Abyss walker. Fog spitters reduce nearby unlit targeting range; shine the beam on them to clear their mist.

07 / Explore

Click a creature for a close live view and its light response; Esc returns to the colony. WASD and the arrow keys pan the camera. Hold Shift to pan faster, or click the minimap to jump. Z, X, C and V open build categories. Use the displayed numbers to pick a building.

08 / Cooperate

Open your current world to LAN from the menu. One host and three guests share the colony. Each player has a personal building inventory. The host controls time, saves and restarting. Keep the host’s game open.

Sawmills near trees supply wood without using grid power. Generators consume wood and add eighteen power. Under power shortage, the newest machines stop. The swarm timer flashes red in the final twenty seconds. The HUD shows the active and upcoming round bonuses. Worlds save locally every thirty seconds. Before each swarm, a rolling checkpoint is stored; retry it from the menu or defeat screen. Checkpoints are independent of autosave. Recently hit buildings cannot be relocated or dismantled for five seconds. Copper drills and smelters supply circuit assemblers. Steel foundries and component factories supply advanced defenses. Coal mines supply coal generators through colony storage. Warehouses accept finished goods from adjacent machines or conveyors on any side. Deliveries enter shared storage immediately and can be used for construction, fuel and ammunition. Select the output material or choose Adjacent machine recipe to supply a neighboring factory from shared storage. Use R to change the output direction. Deliver to a warehouse or the lighthouse to make materials available for construction. Drag to select buildings, then press M to move them. Invalid moves are blocked; hold Shift to pack invalid buildings after confirmation. Export/import backups from the menu. Old saves migrate in the same Windows app profile.
